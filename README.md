## 🧠 My Approach to AI Development

I believe useful AI applications need more than a connection to a language model.

An effective AI feature should understand its purpose, work with the right information, and behave reliably when information is incomplete.

When developing AI-powered applications, I focus on:

**1. Understanding the business problem**

Identify the task the AI needs to solve and define what a successful result looks like.

**2. Designing the data flow**

Determine where information comes from, how it is processed, and which data the AI can access.

**3. Selecting the right integration**

Choose an appropriate model API or AI architecture based on the product's requirements.

**4. Managing context**

Maintain relevant conversation or workflow context without unnecessarily increasing complexity.

**5. Validating outputs**

Use application logic and appropriate checks to reduce incorrect or unusable responses.

**6. Building for maintainability**

Keep AI functionality modular so models, prompts, tools, and workflows can be improved over time.

**7. Connecting AI to real workflows**

Integrate AI into existing applications, dashboards, APIs, customer interactions, and operational processes.

My goal is to build AI functionality that businesses can actually use.
